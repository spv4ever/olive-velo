Slow · Ride · Live

Where Mediterranean calm meets the spirit of movement

Olive evokes rooted Mediterranean living: warm light, seasonal tables and quiet natural beauty. Velo represents rhythm, exploration and the joy of riding in one of Europe’s most iconic cycling territories.

Together, Olive & Velo creates a premium retreat where performance and serenity coexist — a place designed to ride with purpose, recover deeply, and live each day with intention.

Location

Girona, cycling territory

Girona has become one of Europe’s most influential cycling hubs, known for its year-round riding conditions, diverse landscapes and international cycling culture.

From Quart and Camí de Girona, Olive & Velo offers immediate access to greenways and legendary roads connecting Rocacorba, Els Àngels and the Costa Brava coastline.

  • Girona · Costa Brava
  • Camí de Girona · Quart
  • Direct access to greenways
  • Rocacorba · Els Àngels · Costa Brava routes
